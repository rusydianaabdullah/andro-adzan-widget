## Full description (4000 chars max)

Play accepts a small amount of formatting in the full description: `<b>`, `<i>`, `<u>`, `<br>`
and simple lists. The version below uses plain paragraphs and bullet-style dashes, which
survive every renderer.

```
Adzan Widget computes the five daily prayer times on your phone and calls the adzan when each
one arrives — with no account, no ads, and no internet connection at any point.

The app does not request the INTERNET permission at all. Your coordinates never leave the
device, because there is nowhere for them to go. Prayer times come from the standard
solar-position algorithm running locally, and every adzan recording is bundled inside the app.

<b>Home-screen widget</b>
A dark pill showing the clock, the Hijri and Gregorian date, your city and all six times, with
the current period highlighted. Resize it freely — a narrower layout takes over automatically
when you shrink it. The clock updates itself without alarms, so it costs no battery.

<b>Adzan notifications</b>
Every prayer gets its own notification and its own sound, with a Stop action while the adzan is
playing. Subuh defaults to a recording that includes as-salatu khairum minan-naum. Syuruq
defaults to a short chime rather than a full adzan. You can also pick any audio file on your
phone, or record your own muadhdhin.

<b>Jumuah</b>
Friday Dhuhr can use its own sound and, if your masjid keeps a fixed schedule, its own khutbah
time.

<b>Qibla compass</b>
The bearing to the Kaaba from wherever you are, straight from the phone's sensors. Without a
compass sensor it degrades gracefully to a plain bearing in degrees.

<b>Hijri calendar</b>
A month view pairing Hijri and Gregorian dates, with an adjustment if your local sighting runs
a day ahead or behind.

<b>Calculation you control</b>
- Methods: Kemenag (Indonesia), Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian General Authority,
  University of Karachi, Umm al-Qura (Makkah), MUIS (Singapore), or your own angles
- Asr: Shafi'i or Hanafi
- Per-prayer correction in minutes, so the app can match your local masjid exactly

<b>Location</b>
Read from the phone's own location provider — GPS or network, no Google Play Services — and
only when you press Update location. The city name is a free-text field that belongs to you.

<b>Languages</b>
English and Bahasa Indonesia, following your system setting or chosen inside the app.

<b>Permissions, and why</b>
- Location: to compute prayer times and the qibla bearing. Never transmitted.
- Exact alarms: the adzan has to sound at the computed minute, not whenever the system feels
  like waking up.
- Notifications: to show the prayer notification.
- Microphone: only if you choose to record your own adzan.
- Audio playback in the background: to play the adzan when its time arrives.
```
